# Design fixation

*Designer tendencies*

Signal: Concepts all resemble the first reference you looked at. Counter: Diverge *before* looking at references; do 3 intentionally different directions; bring in a distant-domain analogy.

- **Evidence:** Jansson & Smith 1991 · Strong
